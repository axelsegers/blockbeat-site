Privacy Policy

In short

BlockBeat collects no personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not need an internet connection and has no account.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings, your current game and your best score are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Game Center

BlockBeat has a leaderboard in Game Center. Game Center is a service of Apple, not of BlockBeat. Only if you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone is your best score sent to Apple, together with your Game Center player name, so it can appear on the leaderboard. Apple's privacy policy applies to this: apple.com/legal/privacy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the Settings app of your iPhone. We do not receive any of this data.

Children

BlockBeat is suitable for everyone. Because the app collects no data at all, it also collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
